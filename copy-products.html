Copy products → your uncle's shop

Copies every product (name, ID, barcode, category, photo, location, reorder level) from your NIJA database into your uncle's database. Run this once.

⚠️ Open this file in a normal browser tab (Chrome). It reads from your NIJA project and writes into your uncle's project. It only adds products — if you press the button twice you'll get duplicates.

1 · FROM — your NIJA database (pre-filled)

2 · TO — your uncle's database (paste his config)

Get these from his stock-control.html file (the firebaseConfig block) or Firebase → Project settings.
